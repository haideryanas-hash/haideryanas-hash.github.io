Mutual Fund Overview and InsightsFeatured

Data Analyst Internship Project, Labmentix

Problem: Investors face hundreds of Indian mutual fund schemes and no simple way to compare them.

My role: Cleaned the dataset (data types, missing values, duplicates), built a weighted fund scoring model and designed the dashboard.

Result: Ranked 793 of 814 funds, extracted the Top 30, and delivered a 2-page, 12-visualization Power BI dashboard with an executive summary.

Challenge: 3-year return, expense ratio, fund age and 1-year return sit on different scales, so I normalised them with MinMaxScaler before weighting.

Stock Market Analysis in SQL

Data Analyst Internship Project, Labmentix

Problem: Spot buy and sell moments in six NSE stocks using moving-average signals.

My role: Validated 889 trading days per stock (2015 to 2018) in MySQL, built 20-day and 50-day averages and crossover signals with window functions and CTEs, and made a Streamlit live-SQL app.

Result: 56 Buy and 57 Sell signals across six stocks, plus an insights report.

Challenge: About 50% one-day drops in TCS and Infosys came from unadjusted 1:1 bonus issues. Correcting them changed TCS returns from -23.8% to +52.4% and Infosys from -30.9% to +38.2%.
